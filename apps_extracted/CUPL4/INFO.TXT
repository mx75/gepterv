                     Logical Devices Product Information
                     -----------------------------------


         CUPL-386 - Protected Mode Version

         CUPL-386  will  take  full  advantage  of  the 80386's 32-bit
         registers, 4-gigabyte  physical address  space, and  enhanced
         instruction set.  It executes in the familiar DOS environment
         without  the  need  of  memory  managers  or  other   special
         operating system enhancements.  This means that designs  will
         compile faster and be able to access up to 4 gigabytes of RAM
         instead of just 640K.  In addition, partitioning and multiple
         PLD software is provided as a standard built-in feature.  The
         large PLDs  and FPGAs  are going  to be  supported with  this
         system only  (examples: Altera  Max, AMD  MACH, Xilinx,  Plus
         Logic, Plessey,  Actel, etc.).   Manufacturer specific  place
         and route software and device fitters are not included.


         PLPartition - Multiple PLD Design

         One of the major tasks facing PLD designers is the  selection
         of specific devices to implement a design.  PLPartition takes
         the drudgery out of this process.  This product will  provide
         you with  powerful features  that help  synthesize your logic
         design into one or several PLDs.  Automatic Device  selection
         and design fitting is  accomplished by using a  friendly user
         interface and  extremely fast  fitter technology.   This will
         decrease the time it takes to implement your final design.


         ONCUPL - Schematic Interface

         ONCUPL will interface CUPL to your favorite schematic capture
         software.   You  can  draw  your  schematic from the supplied
         macros and create  a PLD file  directly from your  schematic.
         You can mix graphical and  text entry since ONCUPL creates  a
         PLD source file in CUPL HDL.


         TESTPLA - Automatic Test Vector Generator

         This Automatic Test Vector Generator, designed for 20 and  24
         pin  PLDs,  can  analyze  existing  JEDEC  files, create test
         vectors for maximum coverage  and disassemble them into  CUPL
         source files.


         HOST Platforms of CUPL

         CUPL is available on workstation platforms such as the SUN 3,
         SPARCstation, VAX/VMS,  DEC 3100,  HP9000, and  Apollo DN3500
         and DN4500.   It is also  available on the  various Macintosh
         systems.   CUPL can  integrate into  Mentor Graphics,  Valid,
         Viewlogic,  Racal-Redac,  and  any  other  CAD/CAE UNIX-based
         software environment. An upgrade path is available for  those
         who already own a DOS version of CUPL. 


         ALLPRO-88 - Universal Device Programmer

         The  universal  "DAC-PER-PIN"   programmer,  ALLPRO-88,   can
         integrate with CUPL as a full PLD development workstation. It
         is fully  qualified by  the semiconductor  manufacturers.  It
         can be expanded from  24 to 88 "DAC-PER-PIN"  programming pin
         drivers, or a 200 pin driver combination of "DAC-PER-PIN" and
         "Shared DAC" technology.


         UV-Eraser

         To safely erase  your UV erasable  EPLDs use UV  Eraser Model
         QUV-T8/N.  It can hold up to 15 EPLDs at one time.

         Logical Devices, Inc.  will accept application  notes, design
         examples and useful design  tips for designing with  CUPL PLD
         design software.  All forwarded material will be reviewed and
         become  the  property  of   the  CUPL  User's  Group.    This
         information  will  be  available  for  all CUPL users free of
         charge.  One out of every 10 application notes received  will
         be selected as the best and most useful and the author of the
         application note will be awarded a one-year CUPL  maintenance
         program.
